# Stills provenance

Every image in this folder is a real-time render of the procedural NOIR X1 model
(src/three/watch/buildWatch.ts → public/models/noir-x1.glb), captured by
`scripts/capture-stills.mjs` through the `?still=<pose>` route and encoded to WebP with ffmpeg.
No photography, stock imagery, third-party models or HDRIs are used.
